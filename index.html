The board, day by day

What happens at SSB, and how we train each day

The Services Selection Board runs a fixed five-day procedure. Fly High practises every test on it, in the same order, so nothing on the real days is new.

DAY 1

Screening

  • OIROfficer Intelligence Rating
  • PPDTPicture perception, story & discussion

Timed OIR sets and PPDT narration drills with feedback on every story.

DAY 2

Psychology

  • TATThematic apperception
  • WATWord association
  • SRTSituation reaction
  • SDSelf description

Practice batteries at board speed, reviewed for consistency with your personality.

DAY 3

GTO, part one

  • GDGroup discussion
  • GPEGroup planning
  • PGTProgressive group task
  • HGTHalf group task
  • LECLecturette

Outdoor obstacle work and planning exercises, coached by a former GTO.

DAY 4

GTO, part two

  • IOTIndividual obstacles
  • CTCommand task
  • FGTFinal group task
  • PIPersonal interview

Mock interviews on your own PIQ form, with the follow-up questions officers actually ask.

DAY 5

Conference

  • CONFBoard conference & results

Conference rehearsal so your last impression is calm and clear.

Boards our candidates report to: AFSB Dehradun · Mysuru · Gandhinagar · Varanasi · Guwahati · SSB Allahabad · Bhopal · Kolkata (Navy)
